    3 min

    5 min

  • Prinsengracht & Westerkerk

    The canal ring and the landmark church.

    8 min

    Green space, cafés and cultural venues.

    8 min

    The square and its food and flea markets.

    8 min

  • Dam Square

    The historic centre of Amsterdam.

    20 min

From the archive

The Jordaan developed in the seventeenth century as a working neighbourhood of small homes, workshops and shops. These photographs show some of its earlier street life and buildings.

Getting here & around

With tram and bus stops on the doorstep, the city is easy to explore.

From Centraal Station
Amsterdam Centraal is a short bus ride away.
From Schiphol Airport
Take an NS train to Amsterdam Centraal, then continue by local bus.

Check current stops and diversions with GVB, and airport trains with NS.
